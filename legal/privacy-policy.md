# PRIVACY POLICY
**Effective date: 6 August 2026**
This Privacy Policy explains how personal data are collected, used, stored, disclosed, and deleted in connection with the mobile application ExamAce: SAT® Exam Prep, including its accounts, subscriptions, content, and related services (collectively, the “App”).

The App is created and provided by Weronika Sioda, acting as an individual. Weronika Sioda is the controller of personal data processed directly through the App. In this Privacy Policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Weronika Sioda.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Scope of this Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy applies only to personal data that we receive or process in connection with the App.
Certain services used by the App, particularly Sign in with Apple, the App Store, and Apple’s In-App Purchase system, are operated by Apple. Apple independently determines how it processes information maintained within its own systems. Apple’s processing is governed by Apple’s own privacy notices and terms, not by this Privacy Policy.

We are responsible for personal data that we receive from Apple and store or otherwise process within our systems. We are not responsible for information that remains exclusively within Apple’s systems and that we cannot access or control.

## 2. Personal data we collect
We limit collection to information reasonably necessary to create and maintain accounts, provide educational functionality, verify subscriptions, protect the App, and communicate with users.
### 2.1 Information received through Sign in with Apple
An ExamAce account can be created only through Sign in with Apple. Depending on the choices you make during sign-in, Apple may provide us with:
a unique, developer-specific Apple user identifier;
your name, if you choose to share it;
your email address, if you choose to share it; or
an Apple-generated private relay email address if you use Hide My Email.
The unique identifier allows us to recognise your account without receiving your Apple Account password. We do not receive or store your Apple Account password, full Apple Account profile, iCloud content, contacts, photographs, or other information held in your Apple Account merely because you use Sign in with Apple.
Apple permits users to share either an email address or an Apple-generated relay address and provides developers with a unique identifier for authentication.

### 2.2 Account and educational activity data
When you use the App, we may process information associated with your account, including:
onboarding preferences and programme or course choices;
App settings and notification preferences;
questions attempted and answers submitted;
practice-test and section results;
calculated practice scores;
test dates and test history;
educational progress and completion information;
streaks, achievements, study activity, and similar progress indicators;
study-plan information and recommendations;
referral codes entered, shared, or redeemed;
Auria Coin balances, rewards, redemptions, and related activity; and
other information you deliberately enter or generate through your use of the App.
This information is used to provide the App’s educational features and maintain your progress across sessions and supported devices.
### 2.3 Subscription and purchase information
Subscriptions are purchased and processed through Apple’s In-App Purchase system.
We may receive limited information required to verify and manage access to the App, such as:

the subscription product purchased;
transaction or purchase identifiers;
subscription entitlement status;
purchase, renewal, expiration, cancellation, or revocation dates;
trial or introductory-offer status; and
whether Apple has confirmed an active subscription.
We do not directly receive or store your complete payment-card number, bank-account information, Apple Account payment credentials, or other full payment details. Apple processes your App Store account, payment information, and purchase history under Apple’s own privacy practices.
### 2.4 Communications
If you contact us by email, telephone, or another available support method, we may process:
your name and contact details;
the content of your message;
information required to identify your account;
attachments or screenshots you choose to provide; and
records of our response.
Please do not send health information, government identification numbers, financial credentials, passwords, or other sensitive information unless it is strictly necessary for us to address a lawful request.
### 2.5 Technical, diagnostic, and security information
When the App communicates with our systems, limited technical information may be processed automatically, including:
Internet Protocol address;
date and time of requests;
device type and operating-system version;
App version;
language or regional settings;
authentication and session information;
server logs and request records;
error, crash, and diagnostic information;
suspected fraudulent or unauthorised activity; and
notification permission status and an Apple push-notification token, if you enable notifications.
We use this information only as reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and protect the App.

create, authenticate, and maintain your account;

calculate unofficial practice scores and performance indicators;
generate or present study plans, recommendations, and progress summaries;

establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
We do not sell or rent personal data. We do not use personal data for third-party behavioural advertising or to track users across unrelated applications and websites for advertising purposes.

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

## 9. Account deletion and permanent deletion of data
You may initiate deletion of your ExamAce account through the account settings provided in the App.
Once account deletion is confirmed:

your ExamAce account is permanently deleted;
your access to the deleted account ends;
all personal data stored by us and associated with that account are deleted from our active systems;
your preferences, programme choices, answers, scores, progress, test history, achievements, referral information, Auria Coin records, and other account content are deleted;
the Apple-linked identifier, email address, relay address, or name stored in connection with the account is deleted; and
the deleted progress and account content cannot be restored.
We do not retain a usable copy of a deleted account for marketing, profiling, analytics, or future account reconstruction.
Where our systems create secure backups, residual encrypted copies may remain temporarily until the relevant backup is automatically overwritten or securely deleted. Such copies are isolated from ordinary use, are not used for any other purpose, and will not be restored as an active account. If a backup must be restored for disaster recovery before its deletion cycle is complete, the deletion will be reapplied.

We may retain only the minimum information that we are strictly required to retain by applicable law or that is necessary for the establishment, exercise, or defence of a legal claim. Such information will be separated or restricted, will not be used to continue providing the deleted account, and will be deleted when the applicable requirement ends.

We will also instruct service providers processing account data on our behalf to delete the associated personal data, subject to the same limited legal and backup exceptions.

Deleting an ExamAce account does not delete information independently held by Apple, including your Apple Account, App Store purchase history, payment information, or information Apple is legally entitled or required to retain. We cannot access or delete information held exclusively by Apple.

Deleting your ExamAce account, deleting the App, or disabling Sign in with Apple does not necessarily cancel an active Apple subscription. Subscriptions must be managed or cancelled separately through your Apple Account subscription settings.

Apple requires apps that support account creation to permit deletion of the complete account record and associated data that the developer is not legally required to retain.

## 10. Managing Sign in with Apple
You can review or disable the App’s access to Sign in with Apple through your Apple Account settings.
Disabling Sign in with Apple may prevent you from accessing your ExamAce account. It does not by itself guarantee deletion of personal data already stored in the App’s systems. To delete your ExamAce account and the data associated with it, use the account-deletion function in the App.

Similarly, deleting your ExamAce account does not delete or modify your Apple Account. Requests concerning information held by Apple must be directed to Apple.

## 12. Children and minors
The App is intended for students preparing for the SAT examination. It is not directed to children under 13, and children under 13 must not create or use an ExamAce account.
We do not knowingly collect personal data from a child under 13. If we learn that such data have been collected, we will delete the account and associated personal data unless applicable law requires a different procedure.

A user who is at least 13 but has not reached the age of legal majority where they live may use the App only with the permission and supervision of a parent or legal guardian, as described in our Terms of Service.

Where processing relies on consent and the user is below the applicable age for providing independent consent to an online service, the consent must be given or authorised by the user’s parent or legal guardian. In Poland, this generally applies to consent-based processing involving children below 16.

A parent or legal guardian who believes that a minor has used the App without the required permission may contact us. After appropriate verification, we will delete the account and all associated personal data stored by us.

US law imposes additional requirements where an online service is directed to children under 13 or knowingly collects their personal information.
